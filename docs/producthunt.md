**Name:** Pilothouse

**Tagline (≤ 60):** One window for every coding agent, and your phone

**Description (≤ 260):**
Run Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and opencode side by side in tabs. One Inbox for everything they ask. Answer from your phone or any browser: live terminal, approvals, diffs. End-to-end encrypted, no account. Free and open source.

**Topics:** Developer Tools, Artificial Intelligence, Open Source, Productivity

**Links:** https://usepilothouse.com · https://github.com/aemresrbyk/pilothouse

**First comment (maker):**

Hi Product Hunt! I built Pilothouse because I kept losing track of which agent was waiting for me.

- Every project gets a real terminal tab running the agent you pick, with your own models and flags.
- Everything they ask lands in one Inbox; risky commands (rm -rf, force-push, deploy) take two taps.
- Hit a plan limit? Hand the work to another agent with the context.
- Away from the desk: scan one QR code and use the iPhone app or usepilothouse.com/app. The live terminal, approvals and diffs, end-to-end encrypted through a relay that can't read anything. Several computers in one list, view-only links for a teammate.
- Voice: talk to your agent, or brainstorm an idea out loud and have it write SPEC.md.
- Runs headless on a server too.

It's free and MIT licensed, collects nothing, and never touches your agents' logins. I'd love to hear what you'd want next.
